Frisbee costs are a mix of per team and per player. 

Consider two scenarios.
Scenario 1 two teams of 14 players each
Scenario 2 4 teams of 7 players each

Fees
Topscore - per player, fees are charged per player
AFDA and NSW Ultimate - per player, fees are charged per player


Expenses:
Consumables (Food, water, sunscreen) Expenses - Food is provided per team, each team gets the same basket of food. However it is consumed per player, each person eats a fixed amount. Large teams are losing out, small teams are winning.
Insurance - insurance fees are player, however the costs are per team. Consider the two sencarios. Under both the same amount of insurance fees are paid as there is the same number of players. However in the second scenario players are playing twice as much and therefore twice the rate of injuries and therefore the insurance costs is twice as high. Teams with many players are overpaying insurance, smaller teams are benefitting with this mistmatch of fees and expenses. 
Field Costs (field hire, cones, scoreboards, line marking, gazebo) -  per team
Streaming - per game My assumption is fewer games streams results in a lower expense for streaming
Discs - per player
