 Ultimate is very binary, you switch which direction you are going every point, it means every second point you are going the same direction. This lines up with the score, if you add the score and it is an even number you are on the the side you started the game on, if it is an odd number, you are on the other side. Because people dont track scores as well as they track which side they are starting frim, it is usually the other way around and they use which side they start the point on to figure out if the score is odd or even. 


Except that half time messes that up, half the time. 
If the half time score is even, then all is well, everything is fine. 
Team B will start the second half on the opposite side of where they started the game, which coincides with what happens when a point ends. 
But if the score isnt 

The difference in score is related to the difference in turnovers. If you beat an opponent by two points, it's because you had 1 or 2 fewer turnovers. If you started on offence then it was only one turnover, if you started on defence, you had to get an extra turnover to get a margin of two points.

Except again that half time messes that up, half the time. When a team scores they start on defence the next point, unless the team that started on defence scores the point to take half. In this case they will start on offence and it messes up the nice relationship between score difference and turnover difference. 

If you won by a two point margin, you either a. started on offence and had one fewer turnover, b. started on defence, had one less turnover and took half time or c. started on defence, had two fewer turnovers and didnt take half.
