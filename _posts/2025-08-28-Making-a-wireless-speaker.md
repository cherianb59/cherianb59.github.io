## Making a wireless speaker

Someone threw out a pair of speakers connected, there was a larger speaker and a tweeter. There was very crude filtering for the tweeter, a single capcitor connected in series. 

I took the speakers and started to design a bluetooth speaker. I love watching DIYPerks, a great youtube channel about making stuff. A decade ago, Matt, the presenter, made a wireless speaker. What I took away from it was that the electronics was relatively easy to connect.

THe hard part of the build is making things look good.

I desoldered all the speakers and cut out holes in the backside of on the boxes to fit all speakers in. I saved the screws and that held htem firmly in place. 

Bought an amplifier from aliexpress

3d printed a handle in tinkercad. TinkerCAD is a great program to get started with CAD. Other CAD programs are really hard to get into, Tinkercad is very simple, it's intuitive but very limited. I've picked up some bad habits from it, however I really liked using it. Doing complicated stuff like chamfers is hard, making a change in one component designed early on means a lot of work fixing everything that relies on it. 

Painted with old car paint spray 

Got the powerbrick from the side of the road 

Designed and 3dprinted a battery adaptor.

The next thing I want to make is make a pair of small computer speakers.
