## 1 Week in Chengdu

I visited Chengdu in mid-July 2025, my daughter had just turned 1 and she was to meet her great grand parents (my wife's mother's parents).

I went with my daughter, wife and her mother, the plane took 10 hours and we arrived at 5AM.

Upon arriving we could walk through the airport to the adjoined metro station. The metro station map was amazing, it seemed to cover every inch of chengdu with train lines going all over the city.

Despite being the airport being 80km from the apartment, it only took 1 hour and $2 to reach the metro station closest to the apartment. The metro and metro stations were similar to Sydney, the metro itself was single level, seats facing each other and the metro station was very nice looking. Both are much better than the Sydney trains and stations. 

Upon exiting the metro station near the apartment I was amazed at how quiet the cars were. They were all EVs and they made hardly any noise. I think the scooters and motorbikes were also mostly EVs as they were also quiet, unlike the imitation whippersnippers we have in Sydney.
The roads were wide, but I didnt see any street parking, it made the city look nicer, cars make a lot of visual clutter. THere are heaps of hire bikes and plenty of accompoanying bike paths. Chengdu has a 100km bike path loop around the city, only for bikes not scooters or motor bikes.

High speed rail to Chongqing, a neighbouring megacity 300km with a population of 30 million, took only 90 minutes. A far cry to the 4.5 hour train ride from Sydney to Canberra.

Electronics are also very cheap, the indoor security cameras have great features and are much chepaer than their Australian equivalents. It was very hot everyday, and they had a fan that could be voice controlled, it would also warn you if you were about to walk into the fan. My MIL bought a child's book which could speak. On each page there were 30 pressure sensitive areas (invisible buttons) that would activate a sound, and there were 8 pages in this book. It only cost $10, it would be at least $20 in China.
