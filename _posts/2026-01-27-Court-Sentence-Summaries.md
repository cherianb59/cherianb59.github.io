My latest hobby has been reading cour sentencing summaries. It started when I read a news article about a women that leaked vehicle registration information to a debt collecter. The news article was paywalled, but it had the women's name and when I searched I found the court sentencing summary. This went into a great amount of detail showed that she leaked over 1,000 people's names and addresses and got $50-$100 each time. It also transpired that the person she sent it to passed that information on to someone else, i.e. he was a middleman. 

The amount of detail that the sentencing went into was really interesting and I kept reading the summaries. 

Things that surprised me : 

- How often people dont go to jail even if they committed a violent crime e.g. robbery. Instead they get a suspended sentence, i.e. if they dont commit another serious crime in the next two years after sentencing they dont go to jail. If they do commit a serious crime then they will go to jail for a specified amount of time. 

- Jail time for violent crimes are sometimes quite low e.g. two years. On the otherhand a non violent crime like drug trafficking will get you ~ 10 years in jail. 

- Getting up to 25% discount on the sentence if you plead guilty at an early oppurtunity. 

- Women commit much fewer violent crimes compared to men

- Fraud cases involve making loan applications on behalf of someone or working at a company and paying fake invoices to your own company, very unsophistcated and easy to catch. It makes me think that more sophistcated fraud methods are not being caught. 

- A lot of violent crime offenders have difficult upbrinings, e.g. parental abuse, sexual abuse, mental health issues. 

- Domestic violence is horrific.
