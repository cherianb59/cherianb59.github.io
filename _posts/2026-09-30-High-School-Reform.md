# Maths

## Statiscis

Quantifying and understanding randomness. Something people are bad at (gambling, superstition, mistrusting vaccines, belief in coincidences)

# English

## Writing

Writing, constatnly and being evaluated is how you get better. In the current system you write a piece and it gets minimal feedback from the teacher. You need constant feedback to get better. My candidates are AI or other students, hiring more teachers is expensive. 

## Speaking

Stand up infront of a small group and talk for 3 minutes. Being able to communicate is a very important skill. Currently you prepare a speech and recite it in front of 30 people. This is not what happens in real life. You don't always get the time to prepare.

Talking in front of a small group and having a little time to prepare is better.


## Video analysis

Most content is now consumed via video, but only movies and TV shows are analysed in the syllabus. The techiniques used arent used by old video formats.

# Economics

The economic literacy of people is very low. Trade, investment, productivity, price controls, inflation. These need to be taught so people understand why you cant print money, why you cant make a price control to make lower prices, why trade improves both nations.
