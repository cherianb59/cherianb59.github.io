Pros: 

- Very walkable city. The footpaths are very wide, the cars have to stop to let pedestrains cross at intersections (unless there are traffic lights). There are restaurants, grocery shops and lots of other small shops on every street, you don't have to walk far to reach

- Grid system. SG is set out in a grid system, it makes navigating the neighbourhood very easy, I wish Australian cities used this. 

- Huge selection of foods. A consequence of so many shops is that there are many different places to eat with different styles. 

- Fairly bikeable city. most places that take <15 minutes to drive to will take a similar amount of time cycling. Caveats are that SF is quite hilly and bikes cannot go on freeways.

- Good public transport. There were 9 of us and using the busses, trams and BART (like a suway or metro) was a good way to take us all around the city. We needed the car to do a big shop at a super market, and we had to drive a fair way to get one that had easy parking. 

- Easy to get an ESIM and have data. With data it's easy to get around the city, compared to china even with data you had to get the right choinses apps to naviagte as the popular apps google maps, are blocked. 
 
Cons:

- Area just around the BART station is sketchy as there are a lot of vagrants milling around, selling their wares, smelling badly and having their pants around their ankles. 
  
- Driving is slow, constantly need to stop at intersections, this is the price of having a waklable city. I don't think it's possible to have areas that are easy to drive around in and also easy to walk in.

- Everything is very expensive. I felt very poor and didn't want to spend money to eat. Didn't manage to lose any weight though. Not sure how much of this is becuase SF is a HCOL city vs the AUD USD exchange rate.

- It's quite dirty, the streests are littered and there aren't many bins.

- A lack of public toilets, and the ones that are avilable are quite smelly.

- I was looking forward to a cheese stake philly and we went to Jakes steaks and got a footlong for $19 USD. It was dissappointing, the meat is so finely chopped and mised with the cheese it just feels like a mush. I really like a bit of chew when eating meat.
