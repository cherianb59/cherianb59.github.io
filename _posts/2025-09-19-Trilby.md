## Trilby - The Art of Theft - Completing the master heist with the cream suit

Back in the 2000s video game reviews via youtube very becoming increasingly popular, amongst them Zero Punctuation stood out . 

Zero Punctuation was a long-running series of fast-talking, highly sarcastic video game reviews by Ben “Yahtzee” Croshaw. His reviews had a rapid delivery, and a mix of humor, cynicism, and sharp critique.

While going through Yahtzee's site I found and started playing his game Trilby: The Art of Theft. It’s a 2D stealth platformer where you play Trilby, a gentleman thief. You have to sneak past guards, break into safes, pick locks and complete heists without being seen. It combines puzzle-like stealth gameplay with a light narrative and Yahtzee’s trademark wit. 

There are seven missions and after each mission you get a score based on how much loot you stole, how long you took, how many alarms you tripped and how many guards you tased. Getting a certain score on certain missions unlocks costumes for trilby. 

One of the mecahnics is visibility, if you are in darkness you can't be seen by guards, if you are in partial darkness you can be seen if oyu aren't sticking to the wall, and in full light nothing hides you. 

The cream suit is a costume which increases your visibility, it makes the game much harder. The last mission is just the first six missions put together and there is a reward for completing the mission with the cream suit, a very difficult task.
