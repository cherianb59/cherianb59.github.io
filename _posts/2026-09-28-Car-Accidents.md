I live near a road tunnel entrance, and it is not well designed. 

Leading up to the tunnel is a two-lane road. Ahead, it splits into two separate routes, the free surface road and the toll tunnel. They boath have two lanes of their own.

The sensible design would be having the left lane feed into the free road and the right lane feed into the tunnel. Or even have the right lane go to the free road and let you avoid the tunnel as it costs more and is not as popular as the free road. Instead, the left lane gives you a choice, you can either stay on the free road or enter the tunnel. The right lane traps you directly into the tunnel with no way out.

The tunnel is unpopular, most drivers don't use it. Anyone who finds themselves in the right lane suddenly panics and cuts hard across to the left at the last possible second. It happens constantly. Every day I hear honking at drivers pulling reckless maneuvers to avoid the toll. I’ve even seen someone reverse 20 metres out of the tunnel to avoid the toll.

Despite all this chaos, I have never seen a crash. I didn't even notice the complete lack of car crashes until someone stole a car, drove it straight down my apartment's driveway, and crashed into the side and take out the brick work. It makes me marvel/wonder about the perceived danger of cutting in front of people and the lack of crashes that happen.
