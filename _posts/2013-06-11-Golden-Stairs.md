# GOlden Stairs

Back when I was a grad in Canberra one of my friends Phil propsed doing a 3 day hike from the Golden Stairs in Katoomba to Jenolan caves. I was a fat lazy kids growing up so this didn't appeal much to me, but Phil's enthusiasm is infectious so he convinced me and three other grads to do the hike. One of the grads, Paul, offered up his Honda Civic Type R (a small two door coupe) for the 3 hour trip.

We needed to start the trek early at 5am Paul arrived and I managed to just stuff my backpack and water into the boot along iwth the other four guy's stuff.

We trekked off and our first stop was Goulbourn macdonalds, we enjoyed a hearty meal and then we jetted off again making good time. After an hour Phil realised he left his phone and wallet and the Goulbourn, we had to turn around. Two hours later we were in the same spot, 100km away from the starting point.
