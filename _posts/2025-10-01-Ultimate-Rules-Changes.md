## Ultimate Frisbee Rules I Would Change

Official Rules have to be made for the high level, lower level games can make their own modifications.

I have two issues I want to solve, firstly it's boring to watch because all of the dead time, secondly at a high level there are few turnovers and this leads to a boring game where it's all about keeping possession. I prefer the socring style of badmington where serving gives no advantage, compared to tennis where you start each point by smashing the ball at your opponent and the game is all about getting breaks of serve.

### Stall count 8 seconds

10 seconds is too long, 8 seconds will be more watchable. You’d see more rushed throws, more forced errors, and crucially, more turnovers. More turnovers mean more break opportunities, which are the most exciting moments in any game. This would shift the balance of power just enough to make every possession feel more urgent and contested.

### On a turnover the closest offensive player must pick it up

Games are kinda boring to watch because of the dead time. Dead time comes from getting ready between points, call discussions and setup time after a turnover. All three points have rules around how long this dead time should be, but observers don't really enforce the last one. Here’s the scene: a disc gets blocked and hits the grass. What happens next? The player who made the great defensive play trots back to their position, the new offensive team's best thrower slowly walks over from across the field to pick up the disc. Both teams use this long ass lull to set up their structures. All the chaos and advantage created by the turnover is completely erased. I hate this. 

My change is that the player on the new offense closest to the disc must pick it up and put it into play. No waiting for your star handler. You’re there? It’s your disc. Go.

This would create genuine fast-break moments and punish teams that are slow to transition. You'd see defenders caught out of position and maybe even a big cutter forced to throw the first pass. It would reward awareness and opportunistic play. This is how beginners play instinctively, and honestly, it's a more natural and exciting way to play the game. Less standing around, more playing.

### Change the same time catch rule

The current rule is baffling. If an offensive player and a defensive player catch the disc at the exact same time, the tie goes to the offense. They keep the disc. Why are we rewarding a bad throw?

I would change this completly, this should be a turnover. At the high level defense has to play very hard to get a turnover and if the defense could catch the disc at the same time, the throw was bad and the offense deserves to lose posession.
