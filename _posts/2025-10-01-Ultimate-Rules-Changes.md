## Ultimate Frisbee Rules I Would Change

Official Rules have to be made for the high level, lower level games can make their own modifications.

I have two issues I want to solve, firstly it's boring to watch because all of the dead time, secondly at a high level there are few turnovers and this leads to a boring game where it's all about keeping possession. I prefer the socring style of badmington where serving gives no advantage, compared to tennis where you start each point by smashing the ball at your opponent and the game is all about getting breaks of serve.

### Stall count 8 seconds

10 seconds is too long, 8 seconds will be more watchable. You’d see more rushed throws, more forced errors, and crucially, more turnovers. More turnovers mean more break opportunities, which are the most exciting moments in any game. This would shift the balance of power just enough to make every possessions feel more urgent and contested.

### On a turnover the closest offensive player must pick it up

Games are kinda boring to watch because of the dead time. Dead time comes from getting ready between points, call discussions and setup time after a turnover. All three issues have rules around how long this dead time should be, but observers don't really enforce the last one. Here’s the scene: a disc gets blocked and hits the grass. What happens next? The player who made the great defensive play trots back , the new offensive team's best thrower meanders over from across the field to pick up the disc. Both teams use this long ass lull to set up their structures. All the chaos and advantage created by the turnover is completely erased. I hate this. 

My change is that the player on the new offense closest to the disc must pick it up and put it into play. No waiting for your star handler. You’re there? It’s your disc. Go.

This would create genuine fast-break moments and punish teams that are slow to transition. You'd see defenders caught out of position and poor throwers forced to throw the first pass. It would reward awareness and opportunistic play. This is how beginners play instinctively, and honestly, it's a more natural and exciting way to play the game. Less standing around, more playing.

### Change the same time catch rule

The current rule is baffling. If an offensive player and a defensive player catch the disc at the exact same time, the tie goes to the offense. They keep the disc. Why are we rewarding a bad throw?

I would change this completly and make it a turnover. At the high level defense has to play very hard to get a turnover and if the defense could catch the disc at the same time, the throw was bad and the offense deserves to lose posession.

### Endzone to end zone scores are worth two points

Currently every goal is worth one point, doesn't matter if it's a huck or a 1 meter pass. I would change this so that if a goal is scored and the throwers pivot was inside their defending endzone it's now worth two points. This rule makes hucks worth more, and rewards risk taking, it makes the game more exciting to watch. This rule has to be implmented along with the closet player picks up on a turnover, otherwise it will create too much dead time. 

I chose the defending end zone because this won't require any field changes, no additional lines or cones needed. Slight downside is if there is a turnover in a defending endzone, if the thrower brings it to the front of the end zone this rule won't apply (their pivot is in the central zone).
