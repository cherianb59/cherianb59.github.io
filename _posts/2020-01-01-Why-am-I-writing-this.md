### Why in github

It's free, it's going to last for a long time, I don't have to manage it. I used to have a wordpress site and tinkered around with it, but I couldn't be bothered maintining it whenever my tinkering messed it up.

I came across [chadbaldwin's post](https://chadbaldwin.net/2021/03/14/how-to-build-a-sql-blog.html) on how to setup this type of blog, his blog looked good and it was easy to setup, so I did it.

I like that I can change the content and properties, e.g. the date of the post, via text. I didn't write this in 2020, but I want this to be the first post on the home page.
